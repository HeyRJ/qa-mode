# 3 · Park the open questions

The review and the first epic leave open questions. Don't stop testing for them: put them in one file for the people who can answer.

```text
Don't wait on these. I'll take the open questions to the BA and the devs.

Put every open question so far, from your review and from these test cases, in one Excel file, Open questions for BA and devs.xlsx:
- Columns: ID (OQ-01, OQ-02…) | Question | Story, AC or rule IDs | Blocking or Not blocking | Test cases affected | Who answers (BA or Dev) | Answer | Status
- Leave Answer empty, set every Status to Open, and freeze the header row.

Keep the <epic ID> test cases as they are, and don't write more test cases yet.
```

**Why it works:** "Test cases affected" shows which tests wait on which answer, and "Who answers" sends each question to the right person. The testers carry on with everything that isn't blocked. In the episode, Claude also added a "How to use" sheet with live counts, without being asked.
